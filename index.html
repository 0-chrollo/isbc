About Us

iBhayi Small Business
Chamber

Our Partner

We are Gqeberha's dedicated small business chamber, committed to growing and empowering entrepreneurs across the Nelson Mandela Bay region. Founded on the belief that small businesses are the backbone of our local economy, we bring business owners together to network, collaborate and access the support they need to thrive. From training opportunities and business expos to member perks and a platform that gives your business real visibility. ISBC exists to make sure no small business in the Bay has to grow alone.

How ISBC supports your business

We help local businesses grow through networking, exposure, collaboration and strategic opportunities connecting entrepreneurs across Nelson Mandela Bay with the right people, the right platforms and the right support.

Networking

Connect with fellow entrepreneurs at business breakfasts, Power Hour sessions, expos and networking events designed to build real relationships and open new doors for your business.

Exposure

Get your business seen. Members are showcased across ISBC platforms, events and our business directory — putting your brand in front of the right audience in the Nelson Mandela Bay area.

Membership Benefits

Unlock exclusive member perks including discounted advertising on KingFisher FM, access to business resources, referral opportunities and a community of entrepreneurs invested in each other's success.

Growth & Development

Access workshops, training opportunities and strategic guidance built for small business owners — giving you the skills, knowledge and support to scale your business sustainably in a competitive market.

Since our founding we have connected, supported and championed hundreds of small businesses across Nelson Mandela Bay and we are just getting started.
